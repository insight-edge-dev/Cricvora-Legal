Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Cricvora account and all associated data at any time.

⚠️

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your account, phone number, display name, and favourite teams cannot be recovered.

✓ Recommended — Instant

📱 Method 1: Delete from the App

The fastest way to delete your account. Works instantly — no waiting period.

  1. 1
    Open the Cricvora app on your phone
  2. 2
    Tap the Profile tab (bottom navigation)
  3. 3
    Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen
  4. 4
    Tap "Delete Account"
  5. 5
    Confirm the deletion in the popup — your account is permanently deleted immediately
✉️ Via Email — Processed within 7 days

📧 Method 2: Request by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by email. Include the phone number associated with your account.

Send your request to contact@cricvora.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and your registered phone number in the message body.

✉️ Send Deletion Request

🗂️ What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

Data Type What Happens Timeframe
Phone number Permanently deleted Immediately
Display name Permanently deleted Immediately
Favourite teams Permanently deleted Immediately
Login sessions & tokens Permanently deleted Immediately
OTP history Permanently deleted Immediately
App preferences Permanently deleted Immediately

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion. No data is archived or kept in backup systems beyond 7 days.

💬 Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at contact@cricvora.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.
